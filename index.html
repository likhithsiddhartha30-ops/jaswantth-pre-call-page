Your Homework

Think about these before we talk.

No need to write anything down. Just have honest answers ready. This is how we build a plan that fits your actual life.

  1. Where are you right now?
    Rough weight, how you feel in your clothes, energy through the workday.
  2. Where do you want to be in 90 days?
    Be specific. A number, a shirt size, how you want to look on the next India trip.
  3. What have you tried before, and why did it stop?
    Gym memberships, YouTube plans, diets. What made you quit each time?
  4. What does a normal day look like?
    Work hours, what you eat, when you sleep, weekend potlucks and eating out.
  5. Why now?
    What changed that made you book this call today and not six months ago?
